## 1. Vendor Risk Management / GRC Market

Estimates vary by scope; the cluster is roughly **$7-9B in 2024** growing mid-teens:

- **Grand View Research (2024):** $7.42B in 2023 → $20.59B by 2030, **15.7% CAGR** (2024-2030).
- **MarketsandMarkets (2024):** $8.57B in 2024 → $37.34B by 2035, **14.2% CAGR** (2025-2035).
- **Future Market Insights (2025):** $8.2B in 2025 → $27.4B by 2035, **12.9% CAGR**.
- **Gartner Market Guide for TPRM Technology Solutions** published May 15, 2025 — notes vendors are incorporating ML/AI for automated assessment.

Spread: mid-teens CAGR is consistent; 2024 base is $7-9B.

## 2. MSP Market

- **Canalys/Omdia (2025):** Managed services forecast to grow **13% in 2025 to $595B globally**.
- **MarketsandMarkets (2024):** $365.33B in 2024 → $511.03B by 2029 at **6.9% CAGR** (narrower definition).
- **Techaisle (2024):** SMB + midmarket managed services spending to reach **$104B in 2024**; managed services is a priority for **79% of SMBs** and **97% of upper midmarket** firms.
- **CompTIA State of the Channel:** **64% of SMBs** use at least one external IT service provider (MSP, MSSP, cloud consultant, or break-fix).
- **Datto data (via CompTIA):** Among SMBs with 50-499 employees, **48% use an MSP as primary IT support**, up from 36% in 2022.
- **Datto 2024 State of the MSP Report (1,000+ MSPs surveyed):** Average MSP supports **~122 clients**; majority have fewer than 100.

## 3. SMB Compliance Pain Points

- **SOC 2 Type II audit alone:** SMB range **$12K-$20K**; mid/large range **$20K-$100K** — Vanta, Drata, Workstreet 2024-2025 pricing pages.
- **Compliance automation platforms (Vanta/Drata/Secureframe):** **~$10K floor** for small startups, scaling to low six figures. Typical Vanta progression: **$28K Y1 → $35-40K Y3** — Spendflo, ComplyJet 2025.
- **All-in SOC 2 Type II program cost (platform + audit + remediation):** commonly **$40K-$150K** for SMBs — Sprinto, PUN Group, LowerPlane 2024-2025.
- No defensible primary source found for "% of SMBs filling out questionnaires manually" — see Gaps below.

## 4. Questionnaire / Audit Friction

- **CAIQ v4:** **261 questions** (down from 310 in v3.1). CAIQ-Lite: **124 questions** — Cloud Security Alliance.
- **SIG Core 2025: 627 questions. SIG Lite: ~126-128 questions. SIG Detail: 1,936 questions. SIG Content Library: 1,855 risk-control questions** — Shared Assessments 2025.
- **Time per questionnaire:** **10-40 hours** manually; often multi-week back-and-forth — Steerlab, Carbide, Kioptrix 2024.
- **Sales cycle impact:** the average security review adds **~4 days per clarifying round** to a sales cycle; security-review bottlenecks adding **~2 weeks** to turnaround are directly revenue-costly — Steerlab 2024.
- **Vendor burden:** average vendor spends **23+ hours/week** responding to risk assessment requests — Steerlab 2024.
- **Automation upside ("after" anchor):** vendors using automation report **up to 80%** reduction in response time; one case study cited going from **3 hours to 30 minutes** per questionnaire.

## 6. Breach Cost / Fourth-Party Risk (IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report 2024)

- **Global average breach cost: $4.88M in 2024**, up 10% YoY — the largest single-year jump since the pandemic.
- **Supply-chain / third-party compromises = 15% of all breaches**, with an average cost of **$4.91M**.
- Supply-chain breaches take the **longest to detect and contain at 267 days** — driven by vendor/customer trust exploitation.
- **US average breach cost:** record **$9.36M** in 2024 (IBM via The Record).

## TAM Narrative (for pitch)

> The Third-Party Risk Management software market sits at **~$8.6B today, growing to $37B by 2035** at a mid-teens CAGR. But that's just the tool layer. Underneath it is a **$595B global managed services market** — the MSPs who serve **64% of SMBs**. Today, the compliance work those MSPs do is **manual, painful, and expensive**: a single vendor questionnaire takes **10–40 hours**, SIG Core 2025 alone is **627 questions**, and the downstream cost of a third-party breach averages **$4.91M** and takes **267 days** to detect. Vanta is a **$4.15B** company solving this for mid-market. No one is building this AI-native for the MSPs serving SMBs — that is VerifAI's wedge.

## Gaps / Numbers to Verify Before the Pitch

- **"% of SMBs that fill out vendor questionnaires manually"** — no credible primary stat located. Vendor blogs assert "most" or "80%+" but no defensible source (Whistic/Vanta/Drata primary). Frame as "most SMBs still handle questionnaires manually via spreadsheets" and pull directly from the Whistic 2024 TPRM Impact Report if possible.
- **"Average number of vendor questionnaires a typical SaaS company receives per year"** — no cleanly sourced number. Secondary claims of "multiple per month" exist but no analyst figure.
- **Secureframe 2024/2025 funding round** — no new primary round after Feb 2022 Series B. Public total raised remains ~$79M.
- **Drata 2024 primary funding round** — none public since Dec 2022 Series C; use ARR growth ($59M → $98M per Sacra) instead.
- **MSP client-count figure (122)** — widely cited to "Datto 2024" by secondary blogs; verify directly in the Datto 2024 State of the MSP Report PDF before putting on a slide.
- **% of breaches caused by third parties** — IBM's 2024 figure (15% of all breaches are supply-chain/third-party) is the cleanest; Verizon DBIR and SecurityScorecard report higher percentages with different methodology. Stick with IBM's 15% for defensibility.
